Refunds and Billing Help

Review draft: 16 October 2026. Legal review and store-configuration verification required before publication.

Prices and billing

The existing v1 prices are unchanged. Your Apple App Store or Google Play purchase confirmation shows the localised price, billing period and any eligible offer. Subscriptions renew until cancelled in the store where purchased. RoamTrip uses RevenueCat for subscription management; Apple or Google handles the store payment.

Cancel future renewals

Use Manage subscription in the app or your store account. Cancel before renewal following your store's instructions. Cancellation normally leaves access until the paid period ends, subject to the store's entitlement status. Deleting your RoamTrip account or uninstalling the app does not stop subscription billing.

Request a refund

For Apple purchases, visit https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ and follow Apple's refund-request process. For Google Play purchases, follow https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637. These services decide requests under their applicable policies; we do not promise approval or an invented refund window.

Contact support@roamtrip.mobile if you need billing help, cannot resolve a refund issue, or wish to raise a consumer-rights complaint. Include the store, order/reference number and a short explanation. Do not send full card details, passwords or identity documents.

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits rights or remedies that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law, including Australian Consumer Law. Store refund procedures and any discretionary offer do not remove statutory rights. Cancelling future renewals does not by itself refund previous charges. Refunded or revoked purchases may change premium access according to the store entitlement.

Restore access

If you paid but access is missing, use Restore previous purchase while signed into the store account that purchased the subscription, then contact support if necessary.
