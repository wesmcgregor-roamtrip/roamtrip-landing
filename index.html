What's Inside

Built for the way you travel.

Not another itinerary builder. RoamTrip pulls live data from dozens of sources and surfaces what actually matters, when it matters.

Safety & Intelligence

01

Trip Intelligence

Government-sourced safety scores updated in real time. Weather, air quality, civil unrest, and disaster alerts fused into a single 1–10 risk index.

02

RT-Smart Briefings

A personalised morning briefing delivered every day of your trip — conditions, alerts, and nearby events, waiting before you wake up.

03

Flight Tracking

Real-time flight status for every leg — outbound, return, and multi-stop. Gate, terminal, and boarding status on one timeline.

04

Document Vault

Passports, visas, insurance docs, and PDFs — stored behind Face ID or fingerprint. AI-powered extraction reads your document on import.

Trip Planning & Memories

01

Trip Hub

Your full itinerary in one view. Flights, stays, events, and transport in a scrollable day-by-day timeline that fades past events as you go.

02

Spend Tracking

Log expenses in any currency with live exchange rates built in. Set your home currency once — totals convert automatically, by category.

03

Trip Journal

Write daily notes and attach photos to any day of your trip. Journal photos appear in your trip gallery automatically.

04

Past Trip Archive

Every completed trip becomes a permanent record — animated location trail map, day-by-day event sheet, and a full story view.

05

Dream Trips

Add destinations with no dates — your travel bucket list, inside your itinerary. RT-Smart can analyse and inspire your dreams.

06

Trip Collaboration

Invite fellow travellers to your trip with a single code. Collaborators view and edit in real time, without touching owner controls.

07

Trip Book Export

Turn any trip into a print-ready PDF — full itinerary, events, and details laid out and ready to save or share offline.

Discover & Capture

01

RT-Frame

Know the perfect moment to shoot. Golden hour countdown, blue hour windows, moon phase, and astro conditions tuned to your exact pin and gear.

02

RT-Explore

Point your camera at any place for instant context — restaurants, landmarks, and hidden spots from real-time POI data, plus Wikipedia detail.

03

Destination Phrases

20 essential phrases in 21 languages per destination. Tap any phrase to hear it spoken aloud — no data connection needed once loaded.
