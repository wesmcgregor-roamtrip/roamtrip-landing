Data Storage Policy

Review draft: 16 October 2026. Do not publish until production regions, backup retention, processor retention and deletion operations are verified and this draft is approved.

Where information is stored

Account/profile records, trips, expenses, journal entries and collaboration records use Supabase. Uploaded media and documents use cloud storage. The vault also offers a device-only option: its files are copied to the app document directory, with metadata in SecureStore. RoamTrip does not upload those files through that option. Device operating-system backups and exports can make additional copies; device-only does not mean end-to-end encrypted or excluded from every backup.

The vault uses biometric/PIN access gates and signed URLs for cloud document access. The app does not receive biometric templates. Cloud bucket access policies, encryption at rest and hosting regions must be verified for the deployed environment before security claims are finalised. Session credentials use SecureStore; some profile, itinerary and offline caches use AsyncStorage. These are distinct storage mechanisms.

Retention and deletion

Account and trip data is retained to provide the service until deleted or an account-deletion request is fulfilled. There is no verified blanket 90-day automatic vault expiry. Individual cloud-file deletion removes the file before its metadata, so a failed file removal can be retried.

Request account deletion in Profile → Delete account. The app records a receipt and request date. Requests are processed within 30 days and completion is confirmed by email. Deletion is not immediate on submission. Cloud content you supplied, including content shared with collaborators, is included. Content independently supplied by other users must be handled without deleting their accounts or unrelated data.

Files saved only on a phone must be removed in the Vault on that phone before losing account access; we cannot remotely erase copies on offline devices. Remove exported copies and manage device/cloud backups separately. Any retained records must have a documented lawful purpose and limited retention period. Do not assume that every subscription record requires seven years of retention. Backup and vendor deletion schedules require confirmation before publication; do not promise immediate erasure from every system.

Account deletion does not cancel an Apple/Google subscription. Cancel separately in the purchasing store. For requests without the app, use https://roamtrip.mobile/delete-account.html or support@roamtrip.mobile. Do not email passport scans or passwords.
