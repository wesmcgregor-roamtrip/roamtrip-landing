Privacy Policy

Review draft: 16 October 2026 — pending legal review and deployment verification

RoamTrip is operated by Wes McGregor, an individual sole trader in South Australia, Australia (ABN 75 170 206 234). RoamTrip ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it when you use the RoamTrip mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

Account Information
We collect your account email and the profile details you provide, which may include names, avatar, location/country, sex, age range, currency, travel interests and photography preferences or equipment. These support your account and personalisation.

Trip and Itinerary Data
Information you enter into the App — including destinations, travel dates, flight details, accommodation, events, transport, and budget — is stored to provide the core trip management service.

Location Data
With your permission, the App accesses your device location while in use to provide location-aware features including live safety scores, weather intelligence, golden hour timing, and nearby points of interest. Device permission and feature settings determine location access. Coordinates and photo location metadata may be precise; this policy does not describe all location data as approximate.

Photos and Camera
With your permission, the App accesses your camera and photo library for VibeFrame photo capture and to attach photos to trips and journal entries. Photos and associated metadata, including location where present, are stored for gallery, journal and trip features. Sharing and export features can make your chosen content available to others.

Documents
Uploaded Vault documents use Supabase storage; the device-only option keeps files in the app document directory. The vault uses biometric or PIN access. The operating system handles biometric authentication; RoamTrip does not receive biometric templates. These access gates are not end-to-end file encryption. See our Data Storage Policy for device copies, backups and deletion.

Financial Data
Expense records you enter into the spend tracker are stored in your account. We do not collect, access, or store any banking or payment card information.

Device Information
We use Sentry for crash diagnostics, which may include device/app information, error messages and stack traces. The revised app disables session replay, automatic logs and performance tracing and removes user, request, extra and breadcrumb fields before sending errors. Older installed versions may still use the previous replay configuration until updated. Network infrastructure may process IP addresses. See Sentry’s Privacy Policy.

Push Notification Tokens
If you opt in to notifications, we store a push notification token associated with your account to deliver alerts (safety, flight, weather, events).

AI Feature Context
AI features send relevant profile and trip context to Anthropic. When you use booking extraction, the selected PDF or image and its contents are also sent to Anthropic to extract booking details. Depending on the feature this may include age range, sex, travel/photo preferences, camera gear, destination, itinerary/event details, flight context, weather and nearby places. This context can identify or describe you; it is not guaranteed anonymous. Processing and retention depend on the applicable provider agreement and configuration. We do not promise zero provider retention. See Anthropic’s Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide trip management, intelligence alerts, and itinerary features
  • To send push notifications you have opted in to receive
  • To deliver the RT-Smart daily morning briefing during active trips
  • To process in-app subscription purchases (via RevenueCat)
  • To improve the App through diagnostics and crash reporting
  • To respond to support enquiries

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services to deliver its features. Each service has its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase — Secure cloud database and authentication. Your account data and trip information is stored on Supabase infrastructure.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — Powers AI features including the RT-Smart morning briefing, Photo Scout, and intelligence insights. Profile and trip context described above is sent to generate responses. Provider retention and use depend on the applicable agreement. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps — Mapping, route display, and location search on Android.
  • HERE Technologies — Points of interest discovery and location search.
  • AeroDataBox — Live flight status and schedule data.
  • Open-Meteo — Weather intelligence and forecast data.
  • Unsplash — Destination photography used for trip visuals.
  • Expo and Firebase (Google) — Push notification delivery.
  • Resend — Trip invitation emails, including recipient email and invitation details.
  • RevenueCat — In-app subscription management and purchase verification. Apple or Google processes store payments. RevenueCat receives an app user identifier and purchase/entitlement information to manage access; we do not receive raw payment card details from store billing. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google depending on your device. Their privacy policies apply to payment processing.
  • PDFShift — Converts trip-book content, including itinerary, journal and photo references, into a PDF when you generate a trip book.
  • MapTiler — Satellite and terrain map tiles.
  • Sentry — Crash diagnostics and device/app information. Older app versions may also send session replay and performance data; these are disabled in the revised app described above. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

We only share the minimum data necessary with each third-party service to deliver the relevant feature. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party.

4. Data Security

Cloud requests use HTTPS/TLS. The vault has device biometric/PIN access gates. Cloud access policies, device storage and provider security controls each contribute to protection; these controls do not establish end-to-end encryption. See the Data Storage Policy.

While we take reasonable precautions, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and to keep your device software up to date.

5. Data Retention

We retain account data to provide the service. We process account-deletion requests within 30 days and confirm completion by email. Submission is not immediate erasure. Any legally required retention and backup/provider deletion schedules must be documented in the Data Storage Policy. Device-only files and exported copies require separate removal on the relevant devices.

6. Children's Privacy

RoamTrip is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your data
  • Data portability (receive a copy of your data in a common format)

Account Deletion
Request deletion in Profile → Delete account. The app provides a receipt and date; you do not need to send a separate email for an in-app request. We process deletion within 30 days and email confirmation when complete. For requests without the app, see Account deletion. Deleting an account does not cancel store billing; cancel your subscription separately. Remove device-only Vault files before losing account access.

8. Data handling summary

The sections above describe account/profile, trip, media, document, financial-record, location, purchase, notification and diagnostic data. Data may be sent to service providers and to collaborators when you use sharing features. Store privacy declarations must reflect the released app and each store's definitions; this webpage is not a submitted Data safety form.

9. Cookies and Tracking

Native SDKs can process identifiers and diagnostics without browser cookies. See our Cookies and Tracking Policy for app storage, diagnostics and website services. We do not claim that all diagnostic data is anonymous.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you via the App or by email before the changes take effect. The published policy will identify its effective date.
